AST-48 final DONE requires all its dependency gates, including AST-18. Candidate preparation may proceed separately when explicitly assigned, but no installed-candidate, value-qualified or commercial-ready claim is interchangeable. Missing/manual/blocked evidence remains named and open.

No release, merge, paid execution or trial contact is authorized by this checklist. AST-21/22 are optional only after separate scope/authorization decisions. The current rebaseline is complete when documents and prompts are consistent, not when these future acceptance gates have passed.
